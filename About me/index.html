Summary

UX designer and game designer. I design interfaces and systems for both: ten-plus years across web and editorial design, design systems and enterprise product design, and game design since 2019. I did all the UI and the logo for Dark Island: Faded Memories (on Steam, 40k wishlists), and at Harpia Games I design mechanics, levels and UI. I am drawn to the intricate design challenges game mechanics bring, how they affect the player's perception of the game world and how a game can deliver the most varied, excellent experiences. I have experience managing teams, creating design documentation, working across all areas of game production, and shipping one game demo (PUC Campinas).
