Tiny
Heroes

A landscape mobile game about building a squad of tiny superheroes. Recruit them, train and heal them in a hero headquarters, then send a team of three into a mission. This page covers the interface, from the first wireframes to the final screens.

UI pillars

01

One building, one hub

The hub is a cutaway of a building and every room does one job: recruit, train, heal, research, plan a mission or manage heroes. A top bar keeps the counts you care about in view.

02

The hexagon is the hero

Portraits, powers, the joystick and the pause button share one shape. Teal means ready, yellow a warning and red danger or loss, so state reads before the text does.

03

Brackets, not boxes

Titles and buttons are framed by two corner brackets, red at the bottom left and teal at the top right. It gives a HUD feel without heavy panels over the artwork.

04

Design the bad states

Locked, unlocked, claim, not enough herokens, hero down and mission failed each got their own screen, so the tricky moments are not left to code.

The hub

The hub is a cutaway of a base: eight rooms joined by corridors, with the Hall of the Fallen and the Hall of Fame as connectors. Each room opens its own screens, shown with its place in the hub below.

Rooms unlock as you play

After 4 missionsRecovery Room · Hero Recruitment Center
→
After 6 missionsLeague Room · Communication Room

Hub themes

Watch tower (space), cave base, tower base, tech garage, billionaire mansion. Hallways connect the rooms: the Hall of the Fallen counts the times your heroes have fallen, the Hall of Fame the times they have won.

Glossary

Heroics: game currency. Herokens: character currency, to buy heroes. Power Ups: buffs and debuffs. Gadgets: support area attacks. Hero Packs: give herokens.

The same structure in the final look: a night city, deep blue panels, a stencil title face and teal, red and yellow accents.

Mission 7 screens

The HUD keeps the screen clear: portraits and revive on the left, pause on the right, a stick and three powers at the thumbs. Pause, Continue and Failed sit on the same scene in a blue tint, and failure turns it red.

Portrait animation

The hero hexagon in the HUD changes with the hero’s life. Click the portrait to hit the hero. With each hit the outer hexagon frame spins one way and the thin inner lines spin the other way, and five hits take it from healthy to hurt, warning, critical and down. When it says REVIVE, click again to bring the hero back.

  • Hurt
