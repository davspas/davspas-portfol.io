New Shopfloor

The interface of New Shopfloor, the system Compal’s factory teams use to sign in, reach their production modules and manage who can do what. This page covers the access flow, the menus, the users and permissions area and the record tables.

Production line context

This work was part of a larger effort on laptop production line systems, with interfaces for operators at the plant, for managers of the line and for the people in notebook repair and QA.

Old systems, manual steps

The line ran on old systems and convoluted manual processes that could be automated but had never had the time.

Digital and automated

Based on the processes and the apps in use, we made the line digital and automated very manual tasks, such as comparing files and spotting what changed in them.

One pattern

I created style guides and a pattern library in Adobe XD, and brought two old laptop test applications together into the new pattern as automated test apps.

Design pillars

01

Sign in the way the floor works

Biometrics, face recognition or registered data (AD or user ID). People choose what suits their station, and new users are guided to set a password on first access.

02

Rules before errors

Password screens list the rules (8 digits, a capital letter, a number) with dashes, ticks and crosses, and the main button stays disabled until the form is valid.

03

Any module in two clicks

Favourites sit on top, then general settings and production modules as cards. Line menus group tools into Off Line and In Line, with a breadcrumb and a side menu to move around.

04

Permissions you can read

Users and groups are tables with filters. A user shows their groups and modules, and risky actions such as deactivating, resetting a password or deleting a group ask for a plain-words confirmation.

States and feedback

The tricky moments were designed as screens, not left for later.

Confirm risky actions

Clone, deactivate, activate, reset password, delete group: each asks in plain words, with red only on the destructive button.

Say it worked

A green toast confirms access, a new password, a cloned or deleted user, a reset password. No guessing.

Errors on the field

Red label, red icon and a short message on the field itself, and the rule list shows which rule failed.
