# Requirements — Device Recovery (devices, registration and roll-out)

> **Changes (this version):** the table is by **device** (floor and room levels removed); **Faculty** is multi-select with Faculty and Campus grouping and a "Group by campus" switch; new **Roll out** tab to roll out a recovery version to devices (registered devices only), modelled on the recovery roll-out of Faculty-registered devices.

Prototype slice of the device recovery registration requirements. The full set (v0.4, 30 open questions) is kept outside this prototype; ids below (`FR-ST-xx`, `FR-RR-xx`, `FR-SEC-xx`, `OQ-xx`) refer to it, `FR-DP-xx` is new here (roll-out), and open questions live in `qa.md`.

Only the **table-first** proposal is built; **the original wireframe predates the device-based table and the Roll out tab**. The tree-first proposal (option B) was dropped by design decision.

**Purpose.** Give Campus IT Admins visibility of the recovery profile registration per device (IT-managed devices only), so they can trust that the Room Booking Manager registers automatically, a safety valve to force it again ("Send Recovery Request"), and a way to roll out a recovery version to registered devices. The roll-out is modelled on the recovery roll-out of Faculty-registered devices, with devices instead of Building Controllers.

**Priority.** Status visibility is **MUST**. Manual re-register and the recovery roll-out are **SHOULD**.

**Navbar.** The IT-managed / Faculty-registered toggle is set to **IT-managed**, and **Faculty-registered is disabled** (not hidden), with a tooltip saying why: Faculty-registered devices are registered by their Building Controller.

**Sources of truth.** The full requirements set v0.4, the certainty items of the discovery notes, the review decisions behind this version, and, for the roll-out, the Faculty-registered recovery roll-out in Manage Devices. Where they differ, the prototype follows the requirements and the conflict is logged in `qa.md`.

## Status of each requirement in this prototype

**Built** = visible and working in the mock. **Mocked** = the behaviour is shown with sample data. **Not shown** = a server or cross-prototype concern. **Proposed** = not in the original requirements.

## Screen contract

- **Subheader.** Title Device Recovery; multi-select **Campuses** (NORT, HLSD, MEDS; NORT and HLSD selected); multi-select **Faculties** (ENG, SCI, LIB; ENG selected); **Search** (placeholder "Search devices, campuses or faculties"; asset tag, campus, faculty; not MAC); **Group by campus** switch (on); "Results are filtered · Clear Filters" while a counter, a column filter or an arrival filter is active; **Send Recovery Request(s)** on the right (Registration tab only; disabled with 0 ticked), laid out like the Manage Devices header.
- **Tabs** at the left of the sub-header, before the filters (a segmented control): Registration and Roll out. Switching tab resets the sort, keeps the sub-header filters, the column filters and each tab's own ticks.
- **Counters** (Registration) as filter buttons; clicking the active one clears it.
- **Search** by asset tag, campus or faculty.
- **Table (Registration).** Same chrome as the Manage Devices table: edge to edge, sticky header, fixed layout, zebra rows. Columns: select, Device, Recovery profile, Status, Updated at, actions. **Status** is the device's own status; a device that cannot register shows "Cannot register" with a tooltip, an unavailable one shows "Unavailable" with Retry. **Updated at** is the ISO stamp as stored. Devices are ordered (while no column sort is active): Failed, Unavailable, Not registered, In progress, Cannot register, Registered. With the grouping on, rows nest Faculty (only when more than one Faculty is selected) and Campus (switch on); each level steps in the same distance, group rows show a device count and device totals (icon and number, in five fixed slots) and collapse. **Row actions**, left to right: "See in Manage Devices" icon button (opens Manage Devices for the device's room) and "Send Recovery Request" icon button; both have a hover tooltip: the name when enabled, the name plus the reason when the send icon is disabled. While a send is in flight the enabled send icons (Failed and Not-registered devices) and the sub-header button show as disabled (tooltip "Send Recovery Request. A send is in progress."; the other disabled icons keep their own reason; Registered devices have no icon); read-only mode shows only the Manage Devices icon.
- **Table (Roll out).** Same grouping, search and column filters (not the counters). Columns: select, Device, Recovery profile, Recovery version, Last rolled out, history button; Recovery version and Last rolled out sort but have no filter. Group rows show "N of M" with a short suffix: M is the devices in the group and N those that are Registered, not unavailable and not mid-roll-out (the eligible ones).
- **Info message.** An info ribbon (sticky, not dismissible, flush against the top of the table, below the counters on Registration and below the version strip on Roll out) per tab: Registration says the Room Booking Manager registers devices automatically when they ask for their room info and retries failures itself; Roll out says only registered devices can receive a recovery roll-out; a read-only user gets a second ribbon. Success after a send or a roll-out is a toast; partial and error results are banners.
- **Column sort and filters.** Device, Recovery profile and Updated at (Registration), Device and Recovery profile (Roll out): click the header to sort (ascending, descending, off); the filter icon opens a popover with Clear and Apply. Device uses a cascader (Faculty, then Campus, then Devices; any level may stay empty; placeholders "All faculties", "All campuses", "All devices"), profile uses checkboxes (the recovery profile of the device's room), Updated at uses a rolling period measured from now against the device's stamp (All, Today = last 24 hours, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Never updated = no stamp). Status has neither. Sorting Device also orders Faculties and Campuses by name; other sorts order devices inside their group and, with the grouping on, groups follow their first device in the sorted list. An active filter fills the header's filter icon and turns on "Results are filtered · Clear Filters"; the counters ignore column filters, like search.
- **Selection (Registration).** Only devices carry checkboxes; a device whose recovery profile is Not set, No access or blank, or whose status is Unavailable, cannot be selected. Every other device can be ticked, including Registered devices (which have no send icon at all) and In-progress ones (whose send icon is disabled); a batch sends every ticked device except Queued ones, which are skipped and counted. Ticks survive Search and counter filtering (the confirmation and the sub-header label count what is ticked, visible or not). Applying or clearing a column filter un-ticks every device not visible at that moment, whichever filter hides it. A Campuses change un-ticks the devices outside the new campuses; a Faculties change clears all ticks (both tabs). In read-only mode there are no checkboxes. The header checkbox ticks every selectable device in the filtered list, including ones inside collapsed groups.
- **Selection (Roll out).** Only Registered devices that are not unavailable and not mid-roll-out, only after a version is picked; the header checkbox ticks every such device in the filtered list. Clearing the version clears the ticks; changing it to another version keeps them. Ticks survive Search; column filters, a Campuses change and a Faculties change drop them as on Registration. A tick on a device that stops being eligible (for example it was just sent for re-registration) is dropped. The wizard count and Proceed count every tick, visible or not.
- **Confirmation (send).** One pop-confirm (popover anchored to the clicked row icon or to the sub-header button) for one or many devices: how many units, projects and sites, the first six devices, then devices sent and devices skipped.
- **Roll out dialogs.** Summary (large, "Recovery version roll-out"), status (medium, "Roll-out status · <asset tag>"), history (large, "Roll-out history · <asset tag>"), scheduled list (large, "Scheduled roll-outs"), versions (medium, "Recovery versions"), import (large, "Import recovery version", two steps). All close with the header X, a click on the backdrop or Escape (the status, scheduled and versions dialogs also have a Close button, the summary a Cancel, the import a Cancel on step 1 and Back on step 2, the history has no footer); focus moves into the dialog on open and returns to what opened it when that control still exists (after Retry, Roll out or Cancel schedule the page redraws and focus is lost); the summary and the import cannot be closed while their request runs.
- **Arrival from Manage Devices.** `?station=<id>` and/or `?unit=<asset tag>` (for example `?station=nort-dev1&unit=PRJNORT4004`): Campuses and Faculties are set to exactly the device's campus and faculty (the Manage Devices link carries no faculty; the faculty comes from the device), the table opens filtered by the Device column filter (Faculty, Campus, Devices: all devices of the room, or only the device when no room is given or the room id is unknown), the device highlighted and scrolled into view on the Registration tab (once; the Roll out tab has no highlight), and "Results are filtered · Clear Filters" in the sub-header. No chip row and no "Manage Devices" return button; the user clears the filter from the column or the sub-header (only the sub-header Clear Filters also drops the device highlight).
- **Manage Devices side.** Proposed, not wired in the current Manage Devices prototype: selecting one room would show a "Device recovery registration >" link in the settings panel with the room's device statuses (icon and count) beside it, opening this page on that room in a new tab. In the other direction, "See in Manage Devices" opens Manage Devices with the campus, the IT-managed type and the asset tag as query parameters; Manage Devices selects the campus and type and puts the tag in its search box.
- **Other behaviour rules.**
  - Changing **Faculties** clears the ticks and the counter filter and drops the Device column filter; it keeps Search, the other column filters, sort and the grouping switch. Changing Campuses or Faculties shows a short loading state; zero campuses shows "Select campuses", zero faculties "Select faculties".
  - A device's Updated at is its own stamp; "-" when it has none (Not registered devices never have one). A blank recovery profile shows as "Not set".
  - A sixth, orange **Unavailable** counter appears only while at least one device is unavailable.
  - Changing Campuses or Faculties also drops the Device column filter (it may point at something no longer selected); switching to the Roll out tab clears the Updated at filter (Roll out has no such column); the other column filters carry over.
  - Retry on an Unavailable device takes about a second, then the device loads; it also ends any `?state=` demo except `readonly`, which is fixed at page load.
  - The pop-confirm also closes on a click outside it, any scroll outside it, or a window resize; none of these close it while a send is in flight, and the same holds for the dialogs while their request runs: Escape is stopped before the design system's own handler sees it (outside clicks are stopped for the send; the dialogs refuse to close while locked).
  - Manage Devices deep link (proposed): `project`, `site` and `station` are required, `line` narrows the match when present; the room is matched by name; with a valid campus and faculty a deep link is used instead of restoring the last saved selection (it does not overwrite the saved one); the campus and faculty are switched even when the room is not found; only the selection is skipped.
  - Roll out: a roll-out started now shows "Rolling out" for a few seconds, then "Rolled out" (or "Failed" in the `deploy-fail` demo for devices whose asset tag ends in an even two-digit number); a scheduled roll-out stays "Scheduled" until cancelled; cancelling returns the device to its previous roll-out. The sample data has one scheduled roll-out (DRDNORT4010 for v12), several failed ones and devices that were never rolled out.
- **Further rules (Registration).**
  - Group rows (Faculty and Campus) count devices by their **raw status** (the five recovery statuses in five fixed slots), so a Cannot register or Unavailable device is counted there under its stored status; the counters above the table separate Cannot register and Unavailable. Group rows, their device count and totals follow the filtered list; the counters do not.
  - Sorting puts devices with no value (no Updated at, a blank profile) first when ascending; ties keep the data order (a column sort replaces the status order). Search is one substring over asset tag, campus and faculty (lowercase, trimmed).
  - The profile filter lists the recovery profiles of the devices' rooms in the selected Campuses and Faculties (including "Not set"); Apply with all ticked turns the filter off, with none ticked it hides every device. The Device cascader lists only the faculties, campuses and devices in scope.
  - Clear Filters (sub-header) clears the counter, the column filters and the arrival highlight; it does not clear Search, sort, Campuses, Faculties, the switch or ticks, and it does not show for Search alone.
  - Group collapse state (Faculty and Campus keys) is shared by both tabs and survives filter, Campuses, Faculties and switch changes; it never expands for an arrival device.
  - A send un-ticks the devices it sent or skipped, clears an earlier banner, and Updated at is re-stamped at every poll step. In the `partial` demo only the Failed device of EN-101 Lecture Theatre at HLSD (PRJHLSD4032) is rejected (banner only, no toast; its tick stays); Try again after `fsm-down` ends that demo.
- **Further rules (Roll out).**
  - Wizard: step 1 is active until a version is picked, step 2 until a device is ticked, step 3 once both; no version is pre-selected. Retry rolls out the version of the failed attempt again, with no confirmation and no recheck that the device is still Registered; Cancel schedule has no confirmation.
  - A device with a scheduled roll-out can still be ticked; a new roll-out or schedule replaces the pending one (not kept in history). Whether the Faculty-registered roll-out allows this is not confirmed (Q&A 41).
  - Rolling out writes a history entry only when the run finishes; scheduling writes none. The status and history dialogs are snapshots taken when they open. The scheduled count and list cover all devices (not just the selected Campuses and Faculties), soonest first.
  - An imported version gets the latest id plus one, 5 sample rooms and the same two fixed warnings for any file, becomes the latest and clears the previous "Latest" tag; it does not change the picked version or the ticks. The sample versions are v12 (latest, Priya Raman, 14 rooms), v11 (Tomás Herrera, 14 rooms) and v10 (Tomás Herrera, 12 rooms).
- **Demo states** (query string `?state=`): `unavailable`, `fsm-down` (the Room Booking Manager is unreachable), `partial`, `readonly`, `deploy-fail`, `deploy-down` (the roll-out service is unreachable), `import-error` (the import file fails validation).
