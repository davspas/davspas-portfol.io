In short

My scope: all the UI and the logo for Dark Island: Faded Memories, a dark fantasy game on Steam with 40k wishlists.

Key decision: controller first. Every screen works with a gamepad, focus is always visible and the hint bar mirrors the active device. Silver frames and gold selection over flat navy panels keep the UI ornate but readable over a busy 3D world.

UI pillars

01

Ornate, never noisy

Silver frames, gold selection and small diamond tips give the interface a high-fantasy tone, while flat navy panels keep text readable.

02

Readable over a busy world

The HUD sits on foggy 3D scenes. Gold on navy and a strict resource colour code (blue, purple, red) keep it legible in day and night.

03

Controller first

Every screen is driven by A / B / X, LB / RB and LT / RT. Focus is always visible, and the hint bar mirrors the active device.

04

Nostalgic texture

A touch of chromatic aberration and soft scanlines echo the childhood-era look of the Steam pitch, and the player can tune both in Settings.

Settings

A rotating menu with real controls: toggles, sliders and selectors, saved in your browser.

Open Settings →
Controls. Gamepad: D-pad / stick, A select, B back, X drop, LB / RB pages, LT / RT tabs. Keyboard: arrows or WASD, Enter, Esc, X, Q / E pages, Z / C tabs. Mouse: hover and click. Best on a desktop screen; the prototypes are in English.

Brand

The logo uses a gothic serif with a flared initial, over a recurring dragon motif. The UI borrows its mood in the ornate frames and the display type, but stays quieter so the world remains the star. Brand assets appear only on this page, never inside the prototypes.
